### 5. Suggestions to Improve the Resume
1. **Fix the Date Anomaly:** Change "Aug 2025 – Present" to "Aug 2024 – Present" (or appropriate end date) to maintain credibility and ATS date validity.
2. **Add Hard Numbers & Metrics:** Replace vague descriptions with quantified results:
   - e.g., "Managed operations for 3 enterprise clients & 2 university programs simultaneously"
   - "Improved MIS reporting accuracy from 85% to 100% via standardized Excel templates"
   - "Reduced SLA breach rate by 20% through proactive risk tracking and escalation protocols"
   - "Coordinated 15+ concurrent program deliveries with 98% on-time completion"
3. **Consolidate Achievements:** Merge the separate "Achievements" section into the Experience bullets or use them as a brief "Key Accomplishments" header right after the Summary. Avoid duplication.
4. **Clean Formatting:** Remove double spaces in name/location. Use a single, clean font (Arial, Calibri, or Roboto) and avoid tables/columns; stick to plain text or a single-column layout.
5. **Prioritize Experience Over Education:** Since you have 2+ years of relevant professional experience, move the "Professional Experience" section above "Education" to ensure it's the first substantial content ATS scans.
6. **Tailor Keywords:** Review 2–3 job descriptions for target roles. Ensure exact phrasing of required skills (e.g., "Stakeholder Management" vs. "Stakeholder Management & Communication") appears in your Core Skills and bullets.
7. **Strengthen Action Verb Variety:** Ensure every bullet starts with a strong verb (Coordinated, Spearheaded, Optimized, Spearheaded, Spearheaded). Your current bullets are strong, but adding a few "Spearheaded," "Optimized," or "Streamlined" can improve impact.
8. **Consider a "Tools & Technologies" Sub-section:** List specific software (e.g., Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, VLOOKUP, Macros), Power BI, Tableau, Asana/Jira, CRM platforms) if applicable, as ATS often searches for tool-specific terms.

**Final Thought:** This is a solid foundation for an Operations/Program Coordination role. With the date fix, added metrics, and minor formatting cleanup, the resume will be ATS-resilient and highly competitive for mid-level operations roles.
